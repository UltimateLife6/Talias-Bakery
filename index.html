About Talia's Bakery

Talia's Bakery is a family-owned, family-run bakery in the heart of Washington, NJ. We opened our doors to serve our hometown with the kind of baking you remember — authentic Italian pastries, fresh daily bread, custom celebration cakes, and warm welcomes at the counter.

From Italian cookies and muffins to Middle Eastern delights, pita bread, gelato, and specialty coffee, everything is made with pride. Need something special? We welcome custom cake orders and specialty bakery requests — just ask our family.
