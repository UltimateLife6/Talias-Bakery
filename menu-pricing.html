Ordering Information

Policies for custom orders and specialty requests

Order Inquiries

All custom orders must be placed one week in advance to avoid a $25 SQUEEZE IN fee

Your deposit is your order confirmation if your deposit has not been paid your order has not been confirmed or added to my calendar

If you place a squeeze in order the full amount is to be paid to confirm order

Same day fee $35

Deposit & Payments

All orders require a 50% non-refundable deposit of your order total

Once your invoice has been sent you have 24 hours to pay your deposit or your invoice will be cancelled (unless agreed upon otherwise)

Your remaining total will be due 24 hours before pickup/delivery, & MUST be paid through the original invoice link

I do NOT accept CASH or any other form of payment during pickup

Pickup & Delivery

Pickup address will be sent 24 hours ahead of scheduled time after your remaining balance has been paid

Delivery is a $25 within a 20 mile radius from pickup location

an additional $1 will be charged for each mile over 20

Ex: 26 miles away. Delivery Total = $31
